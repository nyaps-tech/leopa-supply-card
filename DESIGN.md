# Design direction

Reading this as an executive personal business card for people exchanging contact details, with restrained print typography. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1, derived from the owner's premium, minimal, no-scroll brief.

- Charcoal and gold follow the supplied business-card reference; the original black/yellow logo is retained without recolouring.
- Georgia gives the person's name a clear printed-card character, while Arial keeps phone numbers, email and actions legible without font downloads.
- Blessing's name is the focal point. The company and logo are subordinate identifiers.
- A single card surface keeps the experience a contact exchange. There are no company sections or website navigation.
- The QR is part of the card, always visible. Its square modules and quiet zone are preserved; SVG keeps edges precise at whole-module sizes.
- Desktop uses the proportions of a physical card. Mobile stacks identity, direct links and the QR with height-based spacing; short landscape views use paired columns.
- `100svh` budgets for mobile browser controls; safe-area insets protect cutouts. No overflow-hiding or internal scrolling is used to conceal content.
- Hover and keyboard focus provide feedback; no animation competes with contact information.
- Original portrait and print collateral are preserved but excluded from the compact card because they would reduce room for identity and contact sharing.
